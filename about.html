About Sarah Mitchell

A decade of waterfront real estate expertise, built on trust, results, and a genuine love for Toronto's lakefront communities.

Your Waterfront Specialist

Experience That Moves With the Market

I started in Toronto real estate over ten years ago with a simple belief: every client deserves an agent who listens more than they talk, explains more than they sell, and fights harder for their outcome than for a quick commission.

Since then, I've helped more than 350 families buy, sell, and invest in homes across the city's most dynamic waterfront neighbourhoods — from the glass towers of Harbourfront to the charming streets of The Beaches and the industrial-chic lofts of Liberty Village.

What sets my practice apart is local fluency. I don't just track the market; I know the buildings, the condo boards, the school catchments, the rental demand, and the micro-trends that affect your home's value. When you work with me, you get strategy, not just listings.
